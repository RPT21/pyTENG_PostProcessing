Image export settings

Applies to the Download button of every plot (and the camera icon in its toolbar) and to "Download all plots". PNG, JPEG and WebP are pixel (raster) images; SVG is vectorized and keeps full quality at any zoom.

2. Custom plot (any signal vs any signal)

Pick one of the selected experiments to see its own signals, choose the X and Y signals and add the series. A plot can combine series from several experiments.

    3. Figures of merit

    Peak-to-peak values are averaged over the cycles saved in CycleData; power is P = V²/Req (Req from LoadsDescription.ods); energy per cycle is the integral of P over each cycle.

    Choose the signals of every selected experiment: the Voltage signal for each one and, for OC/SC experiments, also the Current signal (Voc uses the voltage, Isc the current).
